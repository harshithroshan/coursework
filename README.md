# Coursework

Selected coursework and lab records. Each file is a standalone assignment;
they are grouped here rather than in separate repositories because they do not
belong to one line of research.

## Writing

**Optimism Bias: Why We Think the Future Will Always Favour Us** (2025)
Handbook entry for the *A–Z of Behavioral Science*, Centre for Social &
Behavioral Change — an explanation of optimism bias for a general audience.
→ [`CSBC_Optimism_Bias_Handbook.pdf`](CSBC_Optimism_Bias_Handbook.pdf)

**Cultural, Linguistic and Contextual Considerations for Adapting the DAST-10
in India** (2024)
Week 8 essay for PSY-IS-4158: Validation for Substance Abuse Scale for Indian
Population. On why a substance-use screening instrument cannot simply be
translated: stigma, gender, and the social meaning of the questions all change
what respondents will honestly report.
→ [`DAST-10_Cultural_Adaptation_Essay.pdf`](DAST-10_Cultural_Adaptation_Essay.pdf)

**Regional Synapse Gain and Loss Accompany Memory Formation in Larval Zebrafish**
(2025)
A summary and critical reading of work on synaptic changes accompanying memory
formation in larval zebrafish.
→ [`Zebrafish_Memory_Term_Paper.pdf`](Zebrafish_Memory_Term_Paper.pdf)
